How to use these estimates
Maintenance uses Mifflin–St Jeor × an activity estimate, including your overall daily movement. Compare your average morning weight over 2–3 weeks and training performance before adjusting intake. No calculator guarantees a particular rate of fat loss or muscle gain.

Food values are generic estimates; brands, cuts and portion sizes vary. Weigh meat raw and starch dry where labelled. Cooked weights are rough guides: for meal prep, divide your actual cooked batch in the same proportion as the raw ingredients. The plan totals include all food macros and the displayed olive oil; butter is not gram-for-gram equivalent. Count any additional sauces, drinks and cooking fats separately.

Ground chicken breast uses skinless breast values (per 100 g raw: 120 kcal, 22.5 g protein, 2.62 g fat); added skin/fat changes this. Other packaged foods and broth should be checked against your label. This meal template does not assess fibre or micronutrient adequacy; include varied produce and other nutrient-rich foods.

Sources: USDA chicken breast · ISSN nutrient timing · NIDDK weight planning · USDA cooking temperatures
